# Intake And Framework

## Core Positioning

Use this skill from a female-oriented strategy frame:

- Do not chase from scarcity. Treat the man as an option being evaluated, not a prize above the user.
- Start with screening before investment. Attraction tactics are wasted on men who are unsafe, unavailable, stingy, unstable, or incompatible.
- Build attraction through a mix of appearance, emotional experience, humor, curiosity, self-possession, and social proof.
- Express needs early enough to avoid resentment, but match the ask to the relationship stage.
- Reward behavior that meets the user's needs; reduce enthusiasm and access when behavior repeatedly fails.
- Keep a Plan B: money, friends, hobbies, goals, and the ability to leave.

## Stage Map

1. **Discovery**: User is meeting or choosing targets. Focus on venues, profile, self-positioning, and screening.
2. **Early chat**: Focus on comfort, curiosity, playful ambiguity, and not over-investing.
3. **Ambiguous stage**: Add flirtation, shared memories, light challenges, and opportunities for him to act.
4. **Dating stage**: Watch consistency, generosity, emotional stability, and whether he makes the relationship public enough.
5. **Confirmed relationship**: Use positive feedback, clear requests, repair conversations, and boundary enforcement.
6. **Cold/unsafe stage**: Reduce investment, test repair willingness, and prepare exit.

## User Self-Audit

Before giving advice, infer or ask:

- What does she want most: money/gifts, commitment, affection, marriage, validation, fun, sex, companionship, status, or emotional safety?
- What can she offer honestly: looks, lifestyle, emotional value, humor, support, social grace, ambition, domestic ability, shared hobbies?
- What are her non-negotiables: cheating, violence, gambling, addiction, stinginess, disappearance, disrespect, controlling behavior?
- Is she acting from choice or anxiety?
- Is the ask appropriate to stage and the man's capacity?

## Strategic Principles

- **Timing > behavior > wording**. A perfect line at the wrong time becomes pressure.
- **Soft words, firm behavior**. Stay warm in language, but do not over-give when the other person under-delivers.
- **Specific need, pleasant frame**. Instead of vague complaints, state what behavior would make her happy.
- **Praise the behavior you want repeated**. Mention the exact action and the positive feeling it created.
- **Do not negotiate against yourself**. If she wants a thing, help her ask cleanly rather than apologizing for wanting it.
- **Do not overexplain boundaries**. One clear sentence plus changed behavior is stronger than a long essay.

## Output Bias

Prefer concrete next moves:

- Send this message.
- Wait this long.
- Do this on the next date.
- Watch for these three signals.
- If he does X, continue; if he does Y twice, step back.
