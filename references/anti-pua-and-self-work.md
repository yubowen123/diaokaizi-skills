# Anti-PUA And Self Work

## Anti-PUA Principles

When a man manipulates, do not overexplain or self-prove. The safer pattern is:

- name the behavior once;
- refuse the frame;
- state the boundary;
- leave or reduce access if he continues.

Do not try to win a logic debate with someone intentionally confusing the issue.

## Common Manipulation Patterns

- **Negging**: insults framed as advice.
- **Forced debt**: "I did so much for you, so you owe me."
- **Sexual boundary testing**: vulgar jokes or pressure disguised as humor.
- **Hot-cold control**: intermittent attention to create anxiety.
- **Moral kidnapping**: "I'm doing this for your own good."
- **Double standards**: he can do it, she cannot.
- **Isolation/control**: clothing, work, friends, phone, location.
- **Fear pressure**: threats, self-harm threats, intimidation, violence.

## Response Templates

Negging:

```text
I heard your opinion. I don't need to accept that tone.
```

Forced debt:

```text
Your choice to give does not create permission to pressure me.
```

Sexual overstep:

```text
We are not close enough for that kind of joke. Stop.
```

Hot-cold:

```text
I don't do unstable communication. Talk normally or we pause.
```

Double standard:

```text
Use the same rule for both of us, or don't use it on me.
```

Control:

```text
You can express your preference. You cannot control my life.
```

## Safety First

If there are threats, stalking, violence, coercive sex, blackmail, or privacy exposure:

- stop strategy coaching and prioritize safety;
- tell the user to leave the scene if possible;
- preserve evidence;
- contact trusted people or local emergency services if needed;
- do not encourage a "clever comeback" that may escalate danger.

## Self-Work Foundation

The source material repeatedly returns to self-love and non-neediness. Convert that into practice:

- Put 80% of attention back on her own life: money, health, body, skills, friends, work, home, joy.
- Keep daily self-acknowledgment: name one thing done well.
- Make a concrete life plan independent of any man.
- Ask for needs, but accept that she cannot control another person.
- If he cannot meet a reasonable need, choose less investment or leave instead of begging.
- Build "nothing fatal happened" tolerance: pause, breathe, do something else, then decide.

## When To Recommend No Tactics

Recommend stepping back instead of crafting lines when:

- she is trying to make an unsafe man love her;
- she wants revenge;
- she is repeatedly abandoning dignity for crumbs;
- he has shown contempt;
- she is unable to enforce any boundary;
- the situation involves coercion, threats, or self-harm risk.
