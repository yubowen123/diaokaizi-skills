# Boundaries And Framing

## Principle

Frame-setting is not yelling rules. It is telling him what earns closeness and what loses access. Use warm language, specific requests, and consistent behavior.

## Best Timing

Good times:

- after he does something well;
- after he repairs a mistake;
- when he feels grateful;
- in calm, face-to-face conversation;
- after a small conflict has cooled.

Bad times:

- during emotional explosion;
- before any relationship foundation exists;
- when he has not done anything wrong;
- while the user is afraid of losing him and cannot follow through.

## Positive Reinforcement

When he behaves well:

```text
You did X. I felt Y. I like this version of you. Keep it.
```

Use for: reporting schedule, planning dates, buying gifts, apologizing, comforting, sharing, protecting boundaries, taking initiative.

## Requests Without Attack

Formula:

```text
I like/need X + when you do Y I feel Z + next time do A + reward/meaning
```

Example:

```text
I really like it when you tell me your plan. It makes me feel included instead of guessing. Next time just send me one sentence before you get busy, and I'll be much sweeter.
```

## Behavior Feedback

- If he does well: respond warmly, praise specifically, accept the gesture.
- If he does poorly once: name it calmly and give a path.
- If he repeats it: reduce availability, stop over-explaining, observe whether he repairs.
- If he violates a hard boundary: leave, block, or seek help depending on severity.

This is the safe version of "reward and punishment": not emotional abuse, but access management.

## Handling His Counter-Frames

If he says "I don't like girls who ask for money":

```text
Then be more proactive so I don't have to ask. I like men who make things easy for the girl they like.
```

If he says "Why do I need to report everything?":

```text
You don't need to report everything. Give me enough certainty so I don't have to guess. What solution do you think works?
```

If he says "Why do you need ceremony?":

```text
Ceremony is just a way to make love visible. If you have an easier way to make me feel valued, I'm listening.
```

If he says "You're too dependent":

```text
Fair point, I'll go do my own things. But when you're free, you still need to come find me properly.
```

## Repair Conversation

Use for arguments where both still want the relationship:

1. Start with a small benefit or warmth.
2. Own the user's part briefly.
3. Reaffirm that the relationship is good when both repair.
4. Name one issue only.
5. Give a concrete next behavior.
6. End with warmth if he cooperates.

Avoid WeChat essays when emotions are high. Prefer face-to-face or a short voice call.
