# Screening And Acquisition

## Places To Meet Men

Recommend channels based on the user's personality:

- **Low-social-energy online**: paid or equipment-heavy games, safer real-name dating apps, interest groups, alumni groups, hobby communities.
- **Lifestyle display**: WeChat Moments, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, group chats. Post positive, active, attractive slices of life rather than complaints or emotional hints.
- **Weak ties**: friends of friends, alumni events, colleague networks, family dinners, business meals.
- **Offline interest scenes**: hiking, cycling, running, badminton, skiing, golf, exhibitions, theater, concerts, livehouse, board games, script murder, classes, lectures, industry events.
- **Higher-barrier scenes**: business events, professional courses, upscale gyms, high-end restaurants, wine/whisky bars, high-end residential community activities.

Pick scenes where the user can also enjoy the activity. If she hates the environment, her energy will read false.

## Social Profile

The user's visible life should communicate:

- attractive but not desperate;
- positive and active, not bitter or money-obsessed;
- socially alive, but not chaotic;
- capable of joy without a man;
- tasteful enough to be brought into his world.

Post ideas: outings, sports, books, food made by herself, work milestones, travel, clean selfies, taste-based hobbies. Avoid frequent public venting, long emotional posts, and indirect complaints about men.

## First-Pass Screening

Green flags:

- consistent replies and basic sharing;
- keeps plans and arrives on time;
- treats service workers normally;
- can spend small money without resentment;
- emotionally stable when disappointed;
- respects pace and boundaries;
- has self-discipline or a stable direction;
- has friends and family relationships that do not look chaotic;
- can repair after small conflict.

Yellow flags:

- very charming but vague;
- inconsistent hot/cold behavior;
- refuses all public traces;
- all talk and no action;
- tests sexual boundaries too early;
- complains about every ex;
- makes the user feel she must prove herself.

Red flags:

- violence, threats, smashing objects, self-harm as pressure;
- cheating, gambling, addiction, major deception;
- controlling clothing, work, friends, phone, or schedule;
- sexual pressure before trust;
- humiliating jokes, negging, body shaming;
- disappearing and returning without explanation;
- stinginess mixed with entitlement;
- "only I can tolerate you" style PUA.

## Matching Logic

Do not define "quality man" only as rich or handsome. Match the user to the life she wants:

- wants security and companionship -> choose emotionally available, steady, generous within means;
- wants resources and ambition -> choose career-oriented, generous, disciplined, but accept busier schedules;
- wants romance and attention -> choose expressive and time-rich, but accept possible lower ambition;
- wants marriage -> screen values, family views, conflict repair, fidelity, money habits, and timeline.

Every desirable trait has a cost. Name the tradeoff honestly.

## Early Tests

Use light, ethical tests, not traps:

- Suggest a small paid action: coffee, dessert, ride, small snack.
- Ask values indirectly: views on cheating, exes, marriage, money, family.
- Observe a minor inconvenience: late table, bad weather, schedule change.
- Set a small boundary and see whether he respects it.
- Let him propose the second date after a good first date.

If a man fails a small respectful test with resentment, do not escalate investment.

## Stop-Loss Rules

- Six months with no progress: review value and likely exit.
- Two clear boundary violations: step back hard.
- Any violence, coercion, stalking, or threats: prioritize safety and leave.
- If the user is constantly anxious, losing sleep, or abandoning goals, shift focus to self-work before tactics.
